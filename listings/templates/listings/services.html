Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to common questions about our services

Our property management fees are competitive and transparent. We typically charge 8-10% of monthly rental income, which includes rent collection, maintenance coordination, tenant management, and regular property inspections. There are no hidden fees or surprises.

Property valuations typically take 3-5 business days from the initial inspection. We provide detailed reports with market analysis, comparable sales data, and professional recommendations. Rush services are available for urgent cases.

We serve all 47 counties in Kenya! While we have strong presence in Nairobi, Kiambu, Machakos, Murang'a, Kajiado, and Nyandarua counties, we can provide services in any county upon request. Our network of professionals covers the entire country.

You'll need the title deed, rates clearance certificate, land rent clearance, and any relevant planning approvals. We can help you gather all necessary documentation and ensure everything is in order for a smooth transaction.

Our verification process includes document verification, physical property inspection, legal compliance checks, and market value assessment. Only properties that meet our strict quality standards receive our verified badge.

You can reach us through multiple channels: WhatsApp for instant support, phone calls, email, or by visiting our office. We also have a 24/7 emergency hotline for urgent property management issues.
