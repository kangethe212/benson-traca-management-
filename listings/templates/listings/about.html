Our Story

Founded in 2004 to transform property management in Kenya, Traca Management Services Ltd has been at the forefront of real estate innovation for over 20 years.

We are committed to delivering exceptional service through innovation, professionalism, and unwavering client satisfaction. Our comprehensive coverage spans across Nairobi, Kiambu, Machakos, Murang'a, Kajiado, and Nyandarua counties, ensuring that no matter where you are in Kenya, we're here to serve you.

Our mission is to bridge the gap between property owners, buyers, and tenants by providing transparent, efficient, and reliable real estate services that exceed expectations.

Our Journey

Key milestones in our growth and development

2004 - Foundation

Traca Management Services was founded with a vision to revolutionize property management in Kenya, starting with a small team and big dreams.

2008 - Early Growth

Established our first major office in Nairobi and began expanding our property portfolio across the capital city.

2012 - Regional Expansion

Extended our services to Kiambu and Machakos counties, establishing our presence in key markets around Nairobi.

2016 - Technology Integration

Launched our first digital platform, enabling online property listings and streamlined management processes.

2020 - National Coverage

Extended our services to all 47 counties, becoming Kenya's most comprehensive property management company.

2024 - Innovation & Excellence

Introduced AI-powered property matching, advanced analytics, and mobile-first solutions for enhanced user experience, celebrating 20 years of excellence.
