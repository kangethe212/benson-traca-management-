Our Story

Building dreams and managing properties since 2003

A Legacy of Trust

Founded in 2003, TRACA Management Services Ltd has grown from a small property management firm to one of Kenya's most trusted real estate service providers. Our journey began with a simple mission: to provide exceptional property services with integrity and professionalism.

Over the past two decades, we've helped thousands of clients buy, sell, and manage properties across Nairobi, Mombasa, Kisumu, Nakuru, and other major counties. Our commitment to excellence has earned us a reputation as a reliable partner in Kenya's real estate market.

Today, we continue to innovate and expand our services, always keeping our clients' needs at the heart of everything we do.
