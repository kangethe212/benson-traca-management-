About Us

Redefining Real Estate Services Across Kenya

Our Story

Founded with a vision to transform property management in Kenya, Traca Management Services Ltd has emerged as a leading real estate company committed to innovation, professionalism, and exceptional client satisfaction.

We specialize in delivering comprehensive property solutions across Nairobi, Kiambu, Machakos, Murang'a, Kajiado, and Nyandarua counties, bringing together local expertise with cutting-edge technology to serve our clients better.

Our journey began with a simple yet powerful mission: to make property transactions seamless, transparent, and profitable for all stakeholders in the Kenyan real estate market.
